[ About me ]

            
6 years of design craft, now with AI in the loop.

          


          

            

              
I work AI-native: research, PRDs, visual exploration, mockups and prototypes all run through agents in my IDE. It hasn't changed what I decide, only how fast I get there and how much I can test before committing.

              
It's also why I can take an idea to a working prototype on my own, instead of stopping at a picture of one.

            

            

              
I've designed for healthcare, legal services, HR, real estate, museums, green energy and e-commerce, mostly fields I knew nothing about at first. Learning a domain fast enough to design for it is half the job.
