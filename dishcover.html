Case study · Dishcover

How a culinary app can make cooking at home feel less like work.

Dishcover is an iOS and Android app with easy, step-by-step recipes for every taste, designed end to end: research, user flow and more than 100 screens in light and dark, in three and a half months.

About

Problem

Many people struggle with cooking — whether they feel uninspired, lack experience, or simply don't enjoy spending time in the kitchen. When hunger strikes, they often feel stuck with the same repetitive meals or don't know what to cook with the ingredients they have. This leads to frustration, wasted food, or the temptation to order takeout again.

Solution

Dishcover helps by offering easy, step-by-step recipes that match the ingredients in your kitchen. Whether you're a beginner or just need a quick idea, the app turns everyday cooking into a simple and fun process — no planning, no stress, just tasty results.

User flow

Designed for simplicity, Dishcover guides users through an intuitive journey — from onboarding and exploring recipes to following step-by-step instructions and cooking with confidence.

Wireframes

100+ wireframes were created during the design process, from the allergy picker and the paywall to the step-by-step cooking screens.

Screens

Every flow from the map, designed in light and dark mode from one set of components.

Screen 01

First Launch

Users can skip any step during the first-time experience — no pressure, just flexibility. Login is quick and simple: no passwords, just a phone number and a one-time code. A short personalization step lets users choose their diet, highlight allergens and mark ingredients they'd like to avoid, making every recipe feel just right.

Screen 02

Main Screen

The home screen brings everything together — personalized recipe suggestions, easy access to filters, and a seamless way to jump back into cooking. The carousel opens with a subscription banner, followed by dishes tailored to the user. To keep things fresh, a photo shows several recipes at once: tap a dish in the picture to open it, or see every recipe in the photo as a list.

Screen 03

Recipe Page

Everything you need to cook — in one place: step-by-step instructions, ingredients and tips. Cooking Mode is a hands-free, step-by-step experience that keeps you focused on the dish. If you'd rather stay out of it, the recipe page keeps a simplified list of all steps for quick reference. After cooking, a short rating helps personalize future suggestions.

Screen 04

Search Page

Search finds recipes by ingredients, dish names, or simply exploring ideas. For users who aren't looking for something specific, curated collections offer inspiration. To make the search more tailored, filters cover preparation time, category and dietary preferences.

Screen 05

Favorites & Chef page

Your go-to place for saved content: the Favorites page lets you revisit both your favorite recipes and chefs. When a user likes a recipe, they can add it to a cookbook to keep their favorites neatly organized and easy to find.

Screen 06

User Profile & Subscription

Dishcover relies on a subscription model as its primary way to support the app and provide premium features. Dishcover Plus+ adds exclusive recipes and an ad-free experience, yearly or monthly, each with a free trial. The profile keeps the current plan next to personalization, notifications, language and display settings.
