Privacy

What this site collects, and only if you say yes

This is my portfolio. If you click Accept in the cookie banner, three analytics tools show me how people read it: which pages they open, how far they scroll and where they click. If you click Decline, none of them load.

Who is responsible

Wlad Albakhtsin, the author of this site. Write to hello@wlad.design about anything on this page.

What is collected

  1. 01

    Google Analytics 4, by Google Ireland Limited. Pages you view and for how long, scroll depth, clicks on links to other sites, CV downloads, the site you came from, your rough location (country and city), device, browser and screen size. Google says GA4 does not log or store IP addresses. Kept for 2 months.

  2. 02

    Microsoft Clarity, by Microsoft Corporation. Clicks, scrolling and mouse movement, shown to me as heatmaps and replays of a visit. There are no forms on this site, so nothing you type is recorded. Replays are kept for 30 days, heatmaps for 13 months.

  3. 03

    Contentsquare (formerly Hotjar), by Contentsquare SAS. Heatmaps and replays, like Clarity. Replays are kept for 30 days.

Cookies

With your consent each tool sets its own cookies, on this site and on its own domain. They last from a day to two years. Your choice itself is kept in your browser's local storage, which needs no consent, and the banner asks again after a year.

Where the data goes

Google and Microsoft may process the data in the United States; both are certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. Contentsquare is an EU company. None of it is used for ads or sold to anyone. Each tool's own policy: Google, Microsoft, Contentsquare.

Legal basis

Your consent: Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR and Article 399 of the Polish Electronic Communications Law (Prawo komunikacji elektronicznej).

Your choice and your rights

You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings, here or at the foot of every page. Declining after you accepted deletes the cookies the tools left on this site. You can also ask me to show or delete data about your visits, and you have the right to complain to the Polish data protection authority, Prezes UODO.
